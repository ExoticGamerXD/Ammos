On the menu

Greek classics, done properly

Every dish is prepared fresh to order — the way it's done back home. Move your cursor over a card for a closer look.

Signature
🥙

Pita Gyros

Warm, fluffy pita loaded with slow-cooked meat, crisp salad, tomato, onion and our house tzatziki. The one everyone comes back for.

🍢

Souvlaki Skewers

Char-grilled skewers marinated in Greek herbs and olive oil, served with pita and dips. Simple, smoky and seriously good.

🍟

Loaded Greek Fries

Golden fries topped Greek-style with feta, herbs and sauce — the perfect side, or a meal all on their own.

🥗

Fresh Greek Salad

Tomato, cucumber, olives, red onion and a generous block of feta, dressed in good olive oil. Bright, fresh and light.

🧆

Wraps & Boxes

Build your own box or wrap with your choice of fillings, sauces and sides. Great for on-the-go and big appetites alike.

Sweet
🍯

Greek Sweets

Finish on a high with traditional Greek treats — the honeyed, syrupy kind you won't find anywhere else in Cork.

How it works

Order, buzz, collect

No apps, no queues at a counter — just a simple three-step routine that gets great food into your hands fast.

1
🛎️

Order at the truck

Roll up to the truck, take a look at the menu and place your order with the team. Tell us how you like it.

2
📟

Grab your buzzer

You'll get a buzzer to hold onto. Relax at the tables outside, chat, or take in the fresh air while we cook.

3
🥙

Buzz & collect

When your buzzer goes off, your food's ready. Collect it hot and fresh from the truck — and enjoy.
